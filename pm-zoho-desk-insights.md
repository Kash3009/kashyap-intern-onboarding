# Milestone: Gathering Customer Insights  
## Reviewing Support Tickets in Zoho Desk

---

## Goal

To learn how to systematically review customer support tickets in Zoho Desk, categorise them, and extract meaningful product insights that can inform prioritisation and product decisions.

---

## Why This Is Important

Customer support tickets provide direct, unfiltered feedback from users. They surface recurring bugs, usability issues, and unmet needs that may not appear in analytics alone. For Product Managers, analysing these patterns helps:

- Identify high-impact pain points
- Validate product decisions with real user data
- Prioritise fixes and improvements based on frequency and severity

---

## Research & Learn

### 1. Common Types of Customer Issues in Support Tickets

From reviewing the tickets, the most common issue types were:

- **Bugs / Technical issues** (app crashes, logging issues, platform-specific problems)
- **Feature access & onboarding questions** (especially for organisations or clinics)
- **Security disclosures** (credential exposure, delivery failures)
- **Usability feedback** (confusion around workflows such as log submission)
- **Operational noise** (email bounces, admin or HR-related tickets)

---

### 2. How PMs Use Support Tickets for Product Decisions

Support tickets are used to:

- Detect recurring problems not visible in analytics
- Identify friction points in user workflows
- Feed real user issues directly into engineering backlogs (e.g. GitHub issues)
- Validate whether communication and UX improvements reduce repeat tickets

At Focus Bear, multiple tickets showed a clear support → engineering handoff, demonstrating a mature feedback loop.

---

### 3. System for Categorising User Complaints

For this review, tickets were categorised into:

- **Bugs / Technical issues**
- **Feature requests or access questions**
- **Usability / UX feedback**
- **Security issues**
- **Non-product (admin, HR, scheduling)**

This allowed filtering out noise and focusing on tickets with direct product impact.

---

## Task Execution

### Tickets Reviewed

10 recent, business-relevant Zoho Desk tickets were reviewed and analysed.  
Admin-only and internship-related tickets were excluded from product insights unless they directly affected users or partnerships.

---

### Categorisation Summary

Out of the 10 tickets reviewed:

- **Bugs / Technical issues:** 4  
- **Feature access / partnership requests:** 1  
- **Security-related issues:** 2  
- **Usability / positive feedback:** 1  
- **Non-product / operational noise:** 2  

---

### Recurring Themes Identified

**Theme 1: Confusion Around App Logs and Debugging**  
Multiple tickets referenced “Add App Logs”, platform mismatch (Mac vs Android), or uncertainty about how to submit useful diagnostic information.

**Theme 2: Users Are Willing to Help When Guided Clearly**  
Users showed patience and cooperation when support responses were clear, transparent, and actionable (e.g. linking GitHub issues or explaining next steps).

---

## Deep Dive: Example Ticket Analysis

### Ticket #20281 — *User Feedback: Add App Logs*

**Category:** Bug / Technical issue  

**What was the problem?**  
The user experienced an unexpected app crash and submitted Android logs, even though the issue may have occurred on Mac. This indicated confusion around platform-specific log submission.

**How did support handle it?**  
Support responded quickly, clarified the platform, and provided clear instructions on how to submit Mac logs via the correct in-app flow.

**What could have prevented the issue?**  
- Clear in-app guidance explaining which logs to submit based on platform  
- Platform-aware prompts when users report issues  
- A short help article or tooltip linked from the “Report Problem” flow  

---

## Reflection

### Two Common Pain Points

1. **Unclear debugging and log submission flows**  
   Users were unsure which logs were required or how to submit them correctly.

2. **Lack of self-serve guidance for organisations and advanced users**  
   Clinics and power users required additional clarification during onboarding or troubleshooting.

---

### Patterns in User Frustration

- Frustration decreased significantly when support responses were specific and transparent  
- Users did not mind delays if they understood what was happening next  
- Repeated issues often pointed to UX or documentation gaps rather than product failure

---

### How Focus Bear Could Improve

- Introduce clearer, platform-specific guidance for app log submission  
- Improve self-serve documentation for common issues and onboarding scenarios  
- Add filtering to reduce low-value noise tickets (e.g. email bounces)
- Continue strengthening the support → product feedback loop with consistent tagging and GitHub linkage

---

## Conclusion

This review demonstrated how support tickets can be systematically analysed to uncover recurring product issues, usability gaps, and improvement opportunities. Zoho Desk serves as a valuable source of customer insight when tickets are categorised, filtered, and connected to product workflows.
