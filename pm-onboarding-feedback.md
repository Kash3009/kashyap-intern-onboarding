# Learning from User Onboarding Calls  
**Onboarding Call Insights – Focus Bear**

## Overview

I reviewed and analysed five onboarding call recordings with new Focus Bear users to understand their first impressions, goals, and friction points during the initial experience. These calls provided valuable qualitative context that complements analytics data from PostHog and written feedback from other channels.

Across the calls, users were generally aligned in *why* they came to Focus Bear, but struggled with *how* to get comfortable during the first few sessions.

---

## Summary of Onboarding Calls (5 Users)

### User 1 – ADHD Professional (Remote Worker)
**First impression**
- Liked the calm, supportive tone of the app.
- Felt reassured that the app “wasn’t trying to shame productivity.”

**Pain points**
- Confused by permission requests appearing early without enough context.
- Unsure why some apps were blocked automatically.

**Primary goal**
- Reduce distraction during deep work blocks.

---

### User 2 – University Student
**First impression**
- Found onboarding visually friendly but slightly overwhelming.
- Unsure what to do immediately after logging in.

**Pain points**
- Timer setup felt unintuitive.
- Didn’t understand the difference between Focus Mode and Super Strict Mode.

**Primary goal**
- Stay focused while studying without manually managing distractions.

---

### User 3 – Neurodivergent Creative Freelancer
**First impression**
- Appreciated the positive language and encouragement.
- Liked that the app acknowledged ADHD-specific challenges.

**Pain points**
- Header text and UI spacing felt cramped on mobile.
- The timer selector appeared “unfinished” or abrupt.

**Primary goal**
- Build consistent focus routines without cognitive overload.

---

### User 4 – Knowledge Worker (First Productivity App)
**First impression**
- Curious but cautious.
- Unsure whether the app would be too restrictive.

**Pain points**
- Didn’t fully understand what would happen once a focus session started.
- Wanted clearer preview or explanation before committing.

**Primary goal**
- Gently improve focus without losing flexibility.

---

### User 5 – Returning User (Tried Similar Apps Before)
**First impression**
- Compared Focus Bear favourably to other tools.
- Liked the idea of routines tied to real behaviour.

**Pain points**
- Repeated permission banners felt intrusive.
- Some labels and headings felt technical rather than user-friendly.

**Primary goal**
- Automate focus habits instead of relying on willpower.

---

## Key Themes & Insights

### 1. Strong Emotional Alignment, Weak Initial Clarity
Users consistently resonated with Focus Bear’s *mission and tone*, but struggled with clarity during early setup. The intent of features was clear, but the mechanics were not always obvious.

### 2. Permissions Create Friction
Permission requests were one of the most frequently mentioned pain points:
- Users didn’t always understand *why* a permission was needed.
- Repetition increased anxiety, especially for new users.

### 3. Timer & Focus Setup Is a Critical Moment
Multiple users hesitated or felt uncertain when setting their first focus session. Small UI inconsistencies (scroll behaviour, formatting, spacing) disproportionately affected confidence.

---

## One Small Improvement to Smooth Onboarding

### Suggested Improvement:  
**Add a short, contextual “What happens next?” step before the first focus session begins.**

**Why this helps**
- Reduces uncertainty before committing to a session.
- Sets expectations around app behaviour, permissions, and blocking.
- Matches user desire for predictability, especially for neurodivergent users.

**Example**
A lightweight modal or screen explaining:
- What will be blocked
- How long the session lasts
- How to safely exit or pause
- Reassurance that nothing is permanent

---

## Reflection

### Biggest Struggle During Onboarding
The biggest struggle across calls was *uncertainty*, not resistance. Users wanted reassurance that they understood what the app would do before fully committing.

### One Thing Users Liked
Almost every user mentioned the **supportive, non-judgemental tone** of Focus Bear as a strong positive differentiator compared to other productivity tools.

### One Improvement Based on Calls
Improving early clarity (especially around permissions and focus session behaviour) would significantly reduce onboarding friction without requiring major feature changes.

---

## Final Thoughts

These onboarding calls reinforced the importance of pairing analytics with real user conversations. While data shows *where* users drop off, these calls explain *why*. The insights gathered highlight small, high-impact improvements that can meaningfully improve first-time user confidence and retention.
