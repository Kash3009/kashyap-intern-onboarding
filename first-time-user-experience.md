# First-Time User Experience – Focus Bear App

**Platform:** Android  
**Test Date:** 26 November 2025  
**User ID:** bcc84166-d484-4640-9004-c403bb3780de

This document captures my first-time onboarding experience with the Focus Bear app, approached from the perspective of a brand-new user. The goal was to identify areas of confusion, friction, or improvement during early usage.

---

## App Installation & Initial Navigation

I installed the Focus Bear app on Android and logged in using my email address. I explored the app in the following order:

- Home → Overview  
- Focus tab  
- Stats tab  
- Started a focus session  
- Enabled Super Strict Mode  

Screenshots were taken throughout this flow to capture specific UX issues.

---

## Observations & Issues Identified

### 1. Header Text Clipping (Focus & Stats Pages)

**What I observed:**  
The page titles such as **“Start a focus session”** and **“App & Health Stats”** appear partially cut off at the top of the screen.

**Why this is confusing:**  
This makes the UI feel cramped and unfinished. For neurodivergent users or users sensitive to visual clutter, this can impact readability and trust in the interface.

**Screenshot reference:**  
`screenshot_01_header_clipping.png`

---

### 2. Greeting Uses Full Email Instead of Name

**What I observed:**  
The greeting displays my full email address (e.g. “Hi kashyap01.06.vm…”) rather than a name or nickname.

**Why this is confusing:**  
It feels impersonal and slightly overwhelming. A first-time user expects a friendly, human greeting. Showing an email breaks the warm tone the app otherwise maintains.

**Suggested improvement:**  
Ask for a preferred name or nickname during onboarding and use that in greetings.

**Screenshot reference:**  
`screenshot_02_greeting.png`

---

### 3. Time Selector Formatting & Interaction

**What I observed:**  
The timer selector shows values such as **“55..”**, which looks incomplete. The scroll wheel also stops abruptly instead of looping smoothly.

**Why this is confusing:**  
The formatting looks like a bug, and the interaction feels unnatural compared to native system pickers.

**Suggested improvement:**  
- Use continuous looping scroll  
- Display time values clearly (e.g. “55 min”)

**Screenshot reference:**  
`screenshot_03_timer_ui.png`

---

### 4. Visual Language Inconsistency (Emojis vs Brand Icons)

**What I observed:**  
Some routines use emojis (🧘‍♂️ 🧠 📝) while other parts of the app use a more neutral UI style.

**Why this is confusing:**  
The inconsistency makes the UI feel less cohesive. For a productivity app, a consistent visual language helps reduce cognitive load.

**Suggested improvement:**  
Replace emojis with custom icons aligned to the Focus Bear brand.

**Screenshot reference:**  
`screenshot_04_visual_language.png`

---

### 5. Repeated Permission Warning Banners

**What I observed:**  
Permission warnings appear repeatedly across different screens.

**Why this is confusing:**  
While permissions are important, repeated banners dominate the screen and interrupt the flow, especially during onboarding.

**Suggested improvement:**  
Consolidate permission guidance into a single, collapsible message or step-by-step prompt.

**Screenshot reference:**  
`screenshot_05_permission_banners.png`

---

## Overall Experience

Overall, the app feels friendly, supportive, and aligned with its mission. The tone and intent are strong, especially for ADHD and autistic users. The issues identified are mostly related to polish, spacing, and consistency rather than core functionality.

Improving these early touchpoints would significantly enhance trust and reduce friction during first-time use.
